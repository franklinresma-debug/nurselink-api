NurseLink production scheduler
==============================

Replace API_ROOT and PHP_BIN with the paths returned by `pwd` and `command -v php`.

Laravel Scheduler - every minute
* * * * * cd API_ROOT && PHP_BIN artisan schedule:run >> API_ROOT/storage/logs/cron-schedule.log 2>&1

Run the queue as a supervised service using deployment/systemd/nurselink-queue.service.
